Split Expense ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Split Expense is designed to work locally on your device. We do not require account registration and we do not collect personal information on our servers.

  • Group names you create
  • Member names you enter
  • Expense details (title, payer, amount, split)
  • Calculated summary data (who owes whom)

2. How Data Is Stored

All app data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not transmit your data to any backend server controlled by us.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. If you use the "Export as text" feature, the generated text is copied to your clipboard and can be shared by you through apps of your choice.

4. Third-Party Services

Split Expense does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools in its current version.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Data Security

Since data is stored on-device, security depends on your device protections (passcode, biometrics, OS-level security). We recommend keeping your device protected.

7. Your Choices and Control

  • You can edit or delete app data within the App.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove locally stored data.
  • You control whether exported text is shared with others.

8. International Users

Because data is stored locally and not sent to our servers, cross-border data transfers are generally not performed by us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Effective date" above.
